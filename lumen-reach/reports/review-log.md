# Sun Court iteration record

The first remote render was too sparse: flat triangular backdrop, unarticulated
facade, chunky leaf clusters and a foreground lamp hiding the globe. The next
passes add an original generated watershed painting on opaque silhouette cards,
a sky sphere, smaller individual leaves, facade ribs and a physical entry canopy.
Camera sightlines move the lamp/tree away from the primary composition.

The first clearance audit failed on an incorrect world/local quaternion
conversion. Correct conversion maps the screen's local +Z onto its real physical
front. Subsequent sweeps caught stairs/guardrails crossing the field rigs and
bench approach. The rig positions and bench move into the shelter's open floor.

Exported route checks caught a globe path cutting across stair treads, a route
endpoint incorrectly extending a ramp slope onto its landing, and an annex pier
protruding into the rooftop ascent. Routes now turn along actual landing/ramp
centerlines; supports stay beside the walking surface.

The first nested load rejected a tree-obstructed spawn. Arrival moves to clear
paving. The next load exposed the changing core mount adapter's strict area
shape. Area metadata now uses placement-zone IDs; full AABBs stay in the separate
placement namespace. The owner session is never targeted; all trials use core's
verified controller within the shared nested flock.

Current final evidence is written by the milestone verification commands. No
failed frame is delivered as a completed milestone.
