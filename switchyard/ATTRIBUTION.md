All content: **Hyprune contributors**, [original source](https://github.com/hyprune/worlds/tree/main/switchyard), [CC-BY-4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

This covers all generated geometry, UVs, tile textures, irradiance textures, the original geometric alphabet, signs, blueprint, preview, stills and film. No third-party art, models, image textures, fonts, audio or game assets are imported. The complete editable scene is generated by `build.py` from `world.spec.json`; the procedural seed is fixed. Blender, Cycles and Open Image Denoise are authoring tools, not shipped content. Document changes and retain attribution when redistributing modified content.

The original Switchyard design study informed the project-station metaphor; this package's geometry, alphabet, textures and architectural design were authored anew. Historical Hypr3D scripts were read for remote execution, diffuse-only baking and coplanar sampling technique. No historical geometry, texture, scene or other art file was read or used.

Authoring and validation scripts carry `SPDX-License-Identifier: MIT` and are covered by the repository's LICENSE-MIT. Scripts are not installed inside the immutable data-only package. Package contents retain the content license in LICENSE.
