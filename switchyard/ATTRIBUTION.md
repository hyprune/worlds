All content: **Hyprune contributors**, [original source](https://github.com/hyprune/worlds/tree/main/switchyard), [CC-BY-4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

This covers procedural geometry, UVs, original texture masters and derivatives, irradiance textures, the geometric alphabet, signs, blueprint, previews, stills and film. Every texture is **generated for Hyprune, CC-BY-4.0**. No third-party art, models, fonts, audio or game assets are imported. Scripts are MIT; package contents retain the content license in LICENSE.

`materials/generation.json` records a separate provenance entry and the complete image-generation prompt for each original stone, coated metal, walnut, rubber, brass, cloth, frosted glass and diffuser-panel master. The built-in image generation tool created these specifically for this project. Masters are retained in `materials/source/`. `materials/prepare.py` creates reproducible 1024px periodic tiles: toroidal Poisson boundary healing, narrow periodic edge collars, wrap-safe OpenGL normals, roughness/metallic maps, offset/repeat inspection sheets and an eight-band trim atlas. Walnut is darkened for the final palette; the diffuser receives a phase-aligned procedural cell lattice. Per-master SHA256, modifications, physical tile scale and edge-error evidence are emitted in `materials/tiles/texture-report.json`. Generated package images are derivatives of these masters or completely original numerical palettes/gradients and Cycles lightmaps.

The Switchyard concept study informed the permanent project-station metaphor. The owner's fully original private prototype world informed warm timber bays, bronze detailing, comfortable seating, folded botanical leaves and a city vista. Its original procedural scripts and original renders were studied with the owner's permission; Switchyard's geometry is authored anew, without importing a mesh or texture. Historical remote execution, diffuse-only baking and coplanar sampling scripts were technique references.

Polish 0.1.1 adds an original rounded D glyph with a continuous left stem, an offset exhibit, open ceramic clerestory slats, a procedural three-layer city kit and original architectural landmarks. `build.py` authors two low-energy Gaussian emissive sheen images for the pane and bronze atlas band. Their source, modifications and CC-BY-4.0 attribution are recorded separately in the package texture provenance; no external reflection capture is used.

The complete editable scene is generated by `build.py`, `architecture.py`, `skyline.py`, `lettering.py`, `world.spec.json` and the recorded original texture inputs. Procedural seeds are fixed. Blender, Cycles, Open Image Denoise, Pillow, NumPy and FFmpeg are authoring tools, not shipped package content. Retain attribution and document changes when redistributing modified content.

Authoring and validation scripts carry `SPDX-License-Identifier: MIT` and are covered by the repository's LICENSE-MIT. Scripts are not installed inside the immutable data-only package.
